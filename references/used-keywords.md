Secondary angles in use for resources (don't build a second page around these without differentiating it):

- Tent planning guide: Colorado tent permit thresholds (2021 IFC 400 sq ft vs Colorado Springs and Manitou Springs
  2,400 sq ft, Woodland Park 1,000 sq ft, Monument 400 sq ft); lightning safety for tented events; hail, wind and
  anchoring; tents at altitude.
- Wedding checklist: Colorado wedding rental timeline; questions to ask wedding rental providers; Colorado marriage
  license basics (35 days, self-solemnization, 63-day return).
- Tables and chairs guide: chairs to tables math; banquet vs. theater layout capacity; tent aisle widths and exits (IFC);
  accessible seating at tables.
- Restroom guide: restroom trailer vs. porta potty; how many portable toilets per guest (Mesa County benchmark); ADA
  portable toilet 5 percent rule; handwashing stations.
- Inflatable and party guide: Colorado amusement ride registration for inflatables; Colorado 811 for stakes; city park
  bounce house permit patterns; generators and COI for park events.
- Stage/AV/lighting guide: IFC 3105 temporary special event structure over 400 sq ft; C.R.S. 25-12-103 noise limits;
  stage and amplified-sound permit triggers across Front Range cities.
- Generator/power guide: park outlets not guaranteed; IFC/Brighton Fire generator distances (20 ft from tents, 25 ft
  from public/combustibles, fenced, no refueling while open); generator-triggered permits (Commerce City TEU trailer
  generator, Northglenn NOI, Littleton); cord routing.
- Pavilion guide: shelter booking windows (Broomfield, Englewood, Brighton, Commerce City); group permit headcounts
  (Englewood 15+, Commerce City 25+, Brighton 25+); park bans on bounce houses, canopies, sound, alcohol, glass, staking.
- Block party guide: lead times (Lafayette 10 business days, Golden 21 days, Centennial three weeks, Lone Tree 90 days
  as a special event); neighbor petitions (Lafayette 75 percent); MUTCD barricades and emergency access (Centennial);
  Golden music cutoffs and open container permit; Lafayette insurance 3 business days ahead.
- Insurance guide: host COI vs rental company COI; $1M additional-insured requirements (Golden, Lafayette, Lone Tree
  CGIA limits, South Suburban, Fort Collins, Longmont); deadlines (Golden/Boulder/Manitou 14 days, Lafayette 3 business
  days, Greeley conflict); Colorado Amusement Rides and Devices Program.

- Site plan guide: what Front Range city site plans must show (Westminster, Windsor, Littleton, Woodland Park, Castle
  Pines, Greenwood Village, Thornton); fire district tent drawings (Denver, Brighton, Lafayette, Louisville); measurements
  to get from rental companies; Manitou Springs staged deadlines; Superior 8 1/2 x 11 alcohol diagram.
- Accessibility guide: 2010 ADA Standards for routes (36 in, 1:20, 1:48, 1/4–1/2 in level changes, 1/2 in openings),
  ramps (1:12, 30 in rise, 60 in landings, handrails over 6 in rise), performance-area routes (206.2.6), wheelchair spaces
  table (221.2.1.1); restroom 5 percent and dining 5 percent point to existing guides.
